# karthik-powerpoint-style

Use this skill when creating, revising, or critiquing PowerPoint-style presentation decks in Karthik's visual and analytical style.

## What it is for

This skill turns slide-making into an analytical workflow:

1. Write the slide claim first.
2. Pick the simplest evidence structure.
3. Make the visual large and readable.
4. Annotate the key comparison directly.
5. Remove decoration that does not help the viewer decide what to think or do.

It is meant for analytical decks, management updates, consulting recommendations, product narratives, metric reviews, and chart-heavy presentations.

## How it differs from the chart skills

- `dataviz-selector` chooses the right chart form.
- `karthik-data-visualization` styles the chart itself.
- `karthik-powerpoint-style` turns charts and arguments into slides and deck structures.

In practice, use `dataviz-selector` first when the chart form is unclear, then `karthik-data-visualization` for chart design, then `karthik-powerpoint-style` for the final slide.

## Default slide style

- White background.
- Claim-first title, not topic title.
- One main idea per slide.
- Large central evidence area.
- Sparse colour: grey context plus one accent.
- Direct labels instead of legends.
- Small source/notes footer.
- No decorative stock graphics, gradients, shadows, gauges, pies, 3D charts, or dashboard clutter.

## Example prompt

```text
Use karthik-powerpoint-style to turn these findings into a 6-slide deck outline. Make every slide title a claim, suggest the visual structure, and say what annotation each slide needs.
```
